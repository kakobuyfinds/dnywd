What to check before ordering through CSSBuy

  1. Confirm the variant and measurements

    Check the seller’s size chart for the exact variant. For clothing, compare chest width and length with a garment you own. For shoes, check the seller’s length measurements and sizing notes.

  2. Review warehouse QC photos

    When quality-control photos are available, compare the received item with your selected color and size. Check labels, seams and soles where relevant. Ask about return eligibility before dispatching the parcel if you spot a mismatch.

  3. Estimate the delivered cost

    Start with the item price and add any domestic delivery and agent fees. Compare international shipping options for your destination using the packed weight and dimensions, then account for any applicable taxes or duties.

Frequently asked questions

What is a CSSBuy spreadsheet?

It’s a product directory that helps shoppers find and compare listings they can review before ordering through the CSSBuy shopping agent. This guide uses a selection of products from HipoBuyFind.

Can I order if the CSSBuy option is missing?

If the original seller listing is still available, copy its product URL and paste it into CSSBuy. Confirm that the item and variant load correctly before placing an order.

Why is my CSSBuy total different from the table?

The table shows snapshot prices for the item alone. The seller may have changed the price, your selected variant may cost more, and conversion rates, delivery and fees can affect the amount due.
